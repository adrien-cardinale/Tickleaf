Tasks and projects

Tasks live in an inbox or in a project. A project can be split into sections. Each task has a title, notes, a due date, a priority, an optional repetition and an optional reminder.

Quick add

The field at the top of each view adds a task. Press / anywhere to jump to it. Tickleaf reads the date, the repetition, the priority and the project from the sentence, then removes them from the title.

TypeResult
aujourd'hui, demain, après-demainDue today, tomorrow or the day after.
lundi … dimancheDue on the next such weekday.
dans 3 joursDue in 3 days.
24.12, 24/12, 2026-12-24Due on that date. Without a year, the next occurrence.
tous les jours, chaque semaine, tous les mois, chaque annéeRepeats from today.
tous les 3 jours, toutes les 2 semainesRepeats with that interval.
tous les jours ouvrésRepeats from Monday to Friday.
chaque lundi, tous les vendredisRepeats every week on that day.
p1 … p4, or !1 … !4Priority, from urgent to low. The default is p4.
#ProjectFiles the task in that project.

Example: Appeler la banque demain p1 #Admin creates “Appeler la banque”, due tomorrow, urgent, in the Admin project.

A date in the same sentence sets the first occurrence: Loyer tous les mois 01.11.

Recurring tasks

A recurring task shows a repeat icon next to its date. Completing it does not move it to Terminées: its due date moves to the next occurrence and the task stays open.

  • The next occurrence comes after today. An overdue daily task moves to tomorrow, not to the missed day.
  • A monthly task keeps its day of the month. A task on the 31st falls on the last day of shorter months.
  • A reminder moves by the same number of days, at the same time.
  • Annuler in the notification puts the previous date back.
  • To stop the repetition, choose Ne se répète pas in the task detail.

Views

  • Aujourd’hui: overdue tasks first, with a button to move them to today, then the tasks due today.
  • À venir: the next seven days, day by day, then everything later.
  • Boîte de réception: tasks without a project, waiting to be sorted.
  • Terminées: completed tasks. Restaurer reopens one.

The search field filters the open view. Ctrl+B (⌘+B on a Mac) hides the sidebar.

Projects and sections

  • Add a project from the sidebar. Its menu renames or archives it. Archiving sends its open tasks back to the inbox.
  • Inside a project, Ajouter une section creates a section. Each section has its own add field.
  • A section menu renames or deletes it. Deleting a section keeps its tasks in the project, without a section.

Task detail

Click a task to open its detail. There you change the title, the notes, the due date, the priority, the repetition, the reminder, the project and the section. The trash icon in the header deletes the task.
